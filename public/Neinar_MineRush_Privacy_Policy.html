01 Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Neinar - MineRush (the “Game”), Android package com.neinar.minerush, handles information. It applies to the current release of the Game.

02 Information we collect

The current Game does not require an account, registration, name, email address or phone number to play. The current game code does not transmit gameplay information to a developer-operated server. If you contact us directly, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, which we use to respond to your request.

03 Game data and preferences

The Game stores completed game counts, wins, best completion times by difficulty, and sound and vibration preferences on your device using local application storage. Loss counts and win rates are calculated from these records. These records are used to display statistics and remember your preferences.

04 Advertising and third-party services

The current release does not contain an active advertising SDK. Future releases may introduce advertising or other third-party services, which may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics or approximate location depending on their configuration. Before enabling such services, we will update this policy to describe the actual providers, data practices and available choices, and obtain consent where required. Google Play and your device operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

05 Permissions

The current game features do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Sound and vibration are used for gameplay feedback and can be controlled in Settings.

06 Data sharing and retention

The current game code does not send locally stored gameplay records to third parties or sell them. Local records remain until you reset or remove them, subject to any device backup or restore settings. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to resolve requests or comply with applicable law.

07 Your choices and data deletion

To remove gameplay statistics, open Settings and select Reset statistics. To remove all local game preferences and records, clear the Game’s application storage in Android settings. Uninstalling the Game generally removes local application data, although device backups may retain or restore it. You may contact us to request deletion of information you previously provided to us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

08 Children’s privacy

The current Game does not request personal information from players as part of gameplay. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the Game. Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information. Any future advertising features will be configured in accordance with applicable children’s privacy requirements and the declared target audience.

09 Data security

We aim to protect information handled by the Game and limit data processing to what is needed for its features. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10 Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when game features, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.

11 Contact us

For privacy questions, concerns or data deletion requests, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Game’s Google Play store listing.
