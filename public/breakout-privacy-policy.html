Breakout – Brick Breaker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Privacy First Arcade Game

Breakout – Brick Breaker is developed and published by MeriAsk. It is a classic arcade game where players control a paddle, bounce a ball, break colorful bricks and challenge their best scores across increasingly difficult levels.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of the game handles information, how local data is stored and how you can manage your privacy.

Privacy at a Glance
  • No account or login required
  • No personal information collected through gameplay
  • Best score and feedback preferences stored locally
  • No location tracking
  • No camera, microphone or contacts access required
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs in the current version
  • No sale of personal information
  • Core gameplay works without an internet connection

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Breakout – Brick Breaker does not require registration or an account. The game does not request or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, financial information or advertising identifiers. Gameplay is processed on your device. MeriAsk does not operate a gameplay data collection server for this version.

2. Local Gameplay Data

The game uses local application storage to save your best score and your sound and vibration feedback preference. Current score, level, remaining lives and paddle movements are processed during gameplay. These values are not sent to MeriAsk. The current version does not provide cloud saves or online leaderboards.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to display your best score and remember your feedback setting between sessions. Touch input is used to control the paddle and play the game. We do not use this information to create advertising profiles or track your activity across other apps.

4. Device Permissions and Feedback

The game uses device touch input, system sound and haptic feedback where supported. Vibration may require a normal Android permission. The game does not need access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or shared media to provide its core gameplay. You can turn gameplay feedback on or off from the main menu.

5. Internet and Network Usage

Core gameplay does not require an internet connection. The current game source does not include a network service for sending gameplay information to MeriAsk. Google Play may require internet access for downloading, updating or managing the application.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

MeriAsk does not sell, rent or trade personal information through the current game. The game does not transmit locally stored scores or feedback preferences to advertising networks or analytics providers. If you contact us voluntarily, information you provide may be used to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not contain advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or in-app tracking services. If advertising, analytics or other online features are introduced in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures before releasing that version and obtain consent where required by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services and Device Backups

The game uses Flutter and local preferences storage to provide its functionality. Google Play and your device operating system may independently process information under their own privacy policies. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may back up or restore local application data. MeriAsk does not operate or control those backup services. See the Google Privacy Policy for more information.

9. Children’s Privacy

The game does not require players to submit personal information, regardless of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information directly to MeriAsk, they may contact us to request appropriate action.

10. Data Retention

Best scores and feedback preferences remain in local application storage until they are cleared or removed, subject to device backup and restoration settings. If you contact us by email, we may retain your correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve issues or meet applicable legal obligations.

11. Data Security

We use local application storage and avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. Device security, operating system protections and backup settings also affect the protection of local data. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Deletion and User Choices

You may remove locally stored game data by opening Android Settings, selecting Apps, choosing Breakout – Brick Breaker, opening Storage, and selecting Clear storage or Clear data. You may also uninstall the game. Clearing data resets the saved best score and feedback preference. Device backups may retain or restore information depending on your settings. There is no game account to delete. For information you have voluntarily provided by email, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

13. Purchases and Payments

The current game source does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions or payment processing. MeriAsk does not request payment card or banking information through the game.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when game features, privacy practices or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing new data processing practices.
