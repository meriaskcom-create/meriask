Connect Four – 4 in a Row

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Connect Four – 4 in a Row (the “Game”) is developed and published by MeriAsk. This classic strategy game allows players to drop colored discs into a grid and connect four discs horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Players can compete against another player on the same device or against a built-in computer opponent.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Game handles information, how you can control your data, and how future optional services may affect privacy.

Privacy at a Glance
  • No account, registration, or login required
  • No personal information collected through gameplay
  • Two-player mode works on the same device
  • Computer opponent uses built-in game logic
  • Game scores and moves are held temporarily in memory
  • No location tracking or sensitive device permissions required
  • No advertisements or third-party analytics in the current version
  • No personal data sold or shared through gameplay
  • Future advertising and analytics integrations will be disclosed before release

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, date of birth, physical address, payment information, or other personal details. No account is required to play.

The current version does not contain developer-operated data collection, tracking, or gameplay upload functionality. If you voluntarily contact MeriAsk for support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and handle related support matters.

2. Gameplay Data and Local Processing

During gameplay, the Game temporarily processes the following information on your device:

  • Board positions and red and yellow disc placements
  • Current player and selected game mode
  • Move history used for the Undo feature
  • Round results, wins, losses and draws
  • Session scoreboard and game progress

This information is held in the running Game's memory. The current version does not intentionally save gameplay history or scores in a persistent database or upload them to MeriAsk servers. Session information may be lost when the Game process closes or restarts.

3. Local Two-Player Mode

Two-player mode allows two people to play on the same device. It does not require online matchmaking, player profiles, friend lists, chat, or communication with other devices. Player identities are not collected by this feature.

4. Computer Opponent

The computer opponent uses game logic running directly on your device to choose its moves. It does not send board positions, player moves, or personal information to external AI providers or remote servers.

5. Device Permissions

The Game's core features do not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, shared storage, call logs, or SMS messages. The current Android release source does not request these permissions. Standard operating system functionality may be used to display and run the Game.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

MeriAsk does not sell, rent, or trade personal information through the Game. Gameplay data is not sent to advertisers or other third parties. Information voluntarily provided for support may be handled by our email service provider or disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Internet Usage

Internet access may be used for app distribution, updates, support, viewing this Privacy Policy, and any connected features introduced in future versions. Future advertising or analytics services may communicate with their providers over the internet. The information processed by such services will depend on the actual integrations and applicable privacy choices.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not integrate advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or developer-integrated crash reporting services. It does not use advertising identifiers to personalize advertisements.

MeriAsk may introduce advertising services such as Google AdMob, or analytics and diagnostics services, in future versions. These services are not integrated in the current version. If enabled, their providers may process information such as advertising or device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, app interactions, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information, depending on the service configuration. Such information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, service security, and, where permitted, ad personalization.

Before releasing a version with these services, we will update this policy to identify the actual providers, data practices, purposes, sharing and retention arrangements, and available privacy choices. We will also update Google Play Data Safety disclosures and implement applicable consent and privacy controls. Users may be able to manage advertising preferences through device settings and any consent controls provided in the Game.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game does not require users to submit personal information, including information about children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. If advertising is introduced, its configuration and any applicable age-related protections will be reviewed against the Game’s declared target audience and applicable children’s privacy requirements before release. Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, so that we can review and address the matter.

10. Data Security

The Game minimizes privacy risks by processing gameplay locally and not requiring user accounts or a remote gameplay database. Users should keep their devices and operating systems updated. No method of electronic storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information is retained temporarily during the running session. Starting a new game clears the current board and move history, while session scores may remain until the Game process is restarted. You can remove app-local data by clearing the Game's data in Android settings or uninstalling it.

There is no Game account or server-side gameplay profile to delete. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, resolve issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of personal information you voluntarily provided, subject to applicable law.

12. Third-Party Services and Google Play

The current Game does not integrate third-party services for collecting personal information. Google Play and your device operating system may independently process information for app distribution, updates, security, diagnostics, or other platform functions under their own privacy policies and settings. MeriAsk does not control those independent activities.

13. Purchases and Payments

The current version does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or payment processing. The Game does not request or process bank account details, card information, or other financial information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in applicable store disclosures.
