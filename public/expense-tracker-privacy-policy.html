1. Introduction

MeriAsk respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Expense Tracker (the “App”) handles, stores, uses and protects information. The App helps users record income and expenses, manage monthly budgets and review financial summaries.

2. Information We Collect

The current App does not require registration or login and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, residential addresses, government identification details, precise location, bank account details, card details, UPI credentials or banking passwords.

3. Financial Information Entered by Users

Users may manually enter income and expense amounts, transaction titles, categories, dates, payment method labels, monthly budgets and personal notes. Financial summaries are calculated from these records. This information is processed and stored locally on the device; MeriAsk does not receive it through the App’s expense management functionality. Do not enter passwords, PINs, card numbers or confidential banking credentials into notes or titles.

4. Local Data Storage

The App uses local database storage to save, retrieve, update and delete transactions, manage budgets and generate reports. It does not currently provide developer-operated cloud synchronization or online financial account storage. Operating system backup and restoration may depend on your device settings.

5. How Information Is Used

Locally entered information is used to calculate balances, organize transactions, monitor spending, manage budget limits and display financial summaries and category-wise spending. MeriAsk does not sell or use these records for advertising or marketing.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

The current App does not transmit user-entered financial records to MeriAsk servers or share them with advertisers, financial institutions or other third parties. MeriAsk does not sell, rent or trade these records. If you voluntarily contact us for support, we may use the information you provide to respond, resolve technical issues or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Advertising

The current version does not display third-party advertisements or use advertising SDKs. If advertising is introduced, this policy and applicable Google Play Data Safety declarations will be updated.

8. Analytics and Tracking

The current version does not use third-party analytics SDKs, behavioral tracking or advertising identifiers for profiling. MeriAsk does not monitor individual spending habits. Google Play and Android may independently process technical information under their own policies and user settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Google Play may process information related to installation, updates, compatibility and security. See the Google Privacy Policy. The App does not currently integrate external banking services, payment gateways, financial account aggregators or investment platforms.

10. Device Permissions

The App is designed to operate without access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS or call history. Standard operating system capabilities may be used for local storage and functionality. Any future additional permissions will be disclosed as required.

11. Data Security

Financial records are stored locally. Their security also depends on device protection. Use a secure screen lock, keep your operating system updated, avoid unauthorized device access and install the App from trusted sources. No electronic storage method guarantees absolute security.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Records remain on your device until removed. You can delete individual transactions within the App, clear application storage in Android settings or uninstall the App. Clearing storage generally removes local transactions and budgets. Operating system backups may retain or restore data depending on device settings. There is no developer-operated financial account or cloud database requiring a separate deletion request.

Information voluntarily provided for support is retained only as reasonably necessary to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

13. User Privacy Rights

You may access, review, modify or delete locally stored transactions using available App functionality. You may contact MeriAsk regarding privacy concerns, information provided through support communications or rights under applicable privacy laws.

14. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 years and older and is not specifically designed for children. MeriAsk does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Parents or guardians may contact us if a child has provided personal information directly to MeriAsk.

15. Financial Disclaimer

Expense Tracker is a record-keeping and budgeting tool. It does not provide banking, loans, credit, payments, money transfers, investments, cryptocurrency services, tax advisory services or personalized financial advice. Calculations depend on user-entered information. Users are responsible for its accuracy and their financial decisions. The App is not a substitute for professional financial, legal, accounting or tax advice.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in functionality, legal requirements, security practices or Google Play policies. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Future advertising, analytics, cloud storage or account registration features will be addressed in an updated policy.
