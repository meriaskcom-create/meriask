Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Neinar Color Quest – Memory Game (the “App”) is a color-pattern memory game featuring multiple game modes, difficulty levels, daily challenges, achievements and gameplay statistics. This Privacy Policy explains how MeriAsk (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in connection with the App.

Package name: com.neinar.colorquest · Policy scope: version 2.0.0

Privacy at a Glance
  • No account or registration required.
  • Game records and preferences are saved on your device.
  • No developer-operated game server in the current version.
  • Sharing and external links are initiated by you.
  • No advertising or analytics SDK integrated in the current version.
  • You can reset game statistics and clear stored app data.

1. Information We Collect

The current App does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts or payment information to play. We do not receive gameplay records through a developer-operated game server. If you voluntarily contact us, we may receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include, which we use to respond and resolve your request.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App uses local storage to support gameplay and remember your preferences. Stored information may include:

  • Scores, best scores, completed rounds and accuracy statistics.
  • Game modes, difficulty selections and unfinished game progress.
  • Achievement progress and daily challenge records.
  • Sound, music, vibration and language preferences.

These records are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server. Your operating system may back up app data depending on your device settings.

3. How Information Is Used

Game records are used to restore progress, display statistics, track achievements and personalize settings. Information voluntarily provided to support is used to answer inquiries and troubleshoot problems.

4. Device Features and Permissions

The App uses audio playback for music and sound effects, and optional vibration or haptic feedback for gameplay. These features do not require recording your microphone. Gameplay is not designed to access your camera, contacts or precise location. Sharing and opening links may use system services or other installed apps.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. The current App does not transmit gameplay records to our own servers. If you choose to share a score or app link, the selected receiving app processes the content according to its own privacy practices. Support information may be processed by our email provider or disclosed when legally required.

6. Internet Usage and External Links

Features such as rating the App, visiting external pages, contacting support or sharing content may open Google Play, a browser, an email client or another service. Those services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their independent data practices.

7. Advertising and Analytics

Version 2.0.0 does not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs. If advertising, analytics or related services are introduced in a future release, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure before that release to describe the relevant providers, data categories, purposes, sharing and applicable privacy choices. Any required consent will be obtained where applicable.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses software libraries for local preferences, audio playback, sharing and opening links. Device services and Google Play may independently process information according to their own policies. You can review the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's services.

9. Children's Privacy

The current App does not require registration or knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact us to request its removal. If future services are introduced, applicable children's privacy and platform requirements will be addressed before release.

10. Data Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by the App. Game records are kept using device-provided app storage. Device security, backups and access controls depend in part on your operating system and settings. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Game records remain on your device until you remove them or reset them. You can:

  • Use Reset statistics in the App's Settings to remove game statistics.
  • Clear app storage through Android Settings to remove saved preferences and other local records.
  • Uninstall the App to remove its local app data, subject to operating-system backups.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, resolve issues or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you have sent us, subject to those obligations.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You can change sound, music, vibration and language preferences in Settings, choose whether to share content, and manage device backups through your operating system. You may contact us with questions or requests concerning personal information you voluntarily provided.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements or privacy practices. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
