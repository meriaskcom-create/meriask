1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MeriAsk ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Neinar Car Jam – Bus Escape (the "Game"). The Game is an offline traffic and passenger-matching puzzle game. This policy applies to the version described below and explains your choices regarding information associated with the Game.

2. Information we collect

The Game does not require registration, a user account, or a login. Its current gameplay implementation does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information. We do not operate a server that receives your gameplay progress in this version.

If you voluntarily contact us for support, we may receive your contact details, message, and any information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve issues.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game uses local storage to save information needed for gameplay, including:

  • Level progress, completed levels and current puzzle state.
  • Virtual coins, unlocked vehicle skins and selected appearance.
  • Daily reward and challenge completion dates.
  • Sound, vibration and accessibility preferences.

This information is used to restore progress, manage in-game rewards and personalize settings. It is stored locally through the device's application storage rather than intentionally transmitted to a MeriAsk server. Android backup or device-transfer features may handle local app data according to your device settings.

4. Advertising, purchases and analytics

The current gameplay implementation does not display third-party advertisements, initiate real-money in-app purchases, or implement a developer analytics service. Virtual coins are gameplay rewards and have no real-world monetary value. The project includes advertising and billing software dependencies for potential future use; their presence does not mean that these features are available. If such services are activated, we will review their data practices and update this policy and applicable store disclosures before releasing the changed version.

5. Third-party services

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google may independently process information when you access the store, install or update applications, or use Android services, in accordance with its own policies. We do not control those independent practices. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

6. How information is used and shared

We use locally stored game information to provide game functionality. Support correspondence, if received, is used to respond to inquiries, troubleshoot issues and maintain security. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose support information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect legal rights, safety or security.

7. Device permissions

Gameplay does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. The Game uses device functionality for sound, vibration and local storage. The operating system and included libraries may declare additional technical permissions; permission availability alone does not establish that personal information is collected.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared, overwritten or removed. You can delete local progress by opening Android Settings, selecting Apps, choosing Neinar Car Jam – Bus Escape, and selecting Storage or Storage & cache, then Clear storage or Clear data. This permanently removes local progress and rewards unless a device backup restores them. Uninstalling generally removes local application data, subject to device backup settings.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you have voluntarily supplied, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's privacy

The Game does not require users to submit personal information to play. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact us to request its removal. Children should seek parental or guardian assistance when contacting support.

10. Data security

We aim to minimize the information handled by the Game and use reasonable safeguards for information we receive. However, no storage system or method of communication can guarantee absolute security. We encourage users to keep their devices and operating systems updated.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may exercise applicable rights by contacting us. Because gameplay does not require an account, most game information can be managed directly on your device.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game, third-party services or legal requirements. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions, support or requests concerning your information, please contact MeriAsk using the developer support contact listed on the Game's Google Play store page.
