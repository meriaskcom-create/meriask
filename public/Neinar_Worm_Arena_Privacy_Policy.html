Privacy Policy

Neinar Worm Arena – Snake IO

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MeriAsk (referred to as “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in Neinar Worm Arena – Snake IO (the “Game”), an offline arcade game for Android with package name com.neinar.wormarena.

1. Information we collect

The current offline version does not require an account, registration, name, email address or phone number to play. The Game does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to a developer-operated server during ordinary gameplay.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game uses local storage to save gameplay information, such as coins, experience points, best scores, selected and unlocked skins, and daily reward records. This information is used to maintain progress and personalize your gameplay experience. Local game data may persist between sessions. Depending on your device settings, Android may back up or restore eligible app data.

3. Offline gameplay and permissions

The Game uses computer-controlled opponents and does not require online multiplayer or an internet connection for its core gameplay. The current gameplay code does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or personal media. Android and Google Play may independently process information necessary to install, update or operate apps under their own policies.

4. Advertising, purchases and analytics

The current version covered by this policy does not integrate advertising, in-app purchase or analytics SDKs. In-game coins are virtual gameplay rewards and are not real money. If a future release introduces advertising, paid purchases or analytics, we will update this policy and applicable Google Play disclosures before that release, including information about relevant third-party data practices and available choices.

5. How information is used and shared

Locally stored gameplay information is used to operate the Game, restore progress and provide rewards and customization. We do not sell personal information or share local gameplay records with advertisers in the current version. If you voluntarily contact us for support, we may use the information you provide to respond to your request and resolve technical problems, or retain it where required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared or removed. You can delete it through Android Settings → Apps → Neinar Worm Arena → Storage → Clear storage (the exact menu names may vary). Uninstalling the Game normally removes its local app data, although device backups may retain eligible copies. Clearing storage may permanently reset scores, coins and unlocked skins. For information you voluntarily provide to support, you may request deletion through the contact method below, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

7. Children’s privacy

The current Game does not require players to submit personal information or create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to us may contact us to request its removal.

8. Data security

We aim to limit data handling to what is necessary for the Game. Local data is subject to the protections provided by your device and operating system. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated.

9. Third-party services

If you install or update the Game through Google Play, Google may process information independently. For more information, see Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

You can control local gameplay data through your device settings. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal information you have voluntarily provided to us. Contact us to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the Game, its data practices or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
